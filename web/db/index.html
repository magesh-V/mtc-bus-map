How fast buses move

Observed speed: the road distance a bus covers along its own route between two GPS fixes, over the time between them — stops and signals included, so this is the speed a rider gets. Parked and laid-over buses are left out.

When buses are likely to be crowded

MTC does not publish ticket counts by hour, and the Chennai One app's crowd field is empty. What is real and hourly: when the city travels — Chennai Metro's ticket entries by hour — and how many buses are on the road. Their ratio says when there are the fewest buses per traveller. An estimate, not a passenger count.

How this is collected. A collector polls Chennai One for every running route at 4 requests a second and archives each bus's GPS fix; this page is rebuilt from that archive every hour. Speeds and bunching need two fixes of the same bus, a few minutes apart. Buses that do not report GPS are not counted.
